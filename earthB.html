Earth B is the planet where the Alpha kids reside upon at various points in time. Now if you've seen the Characters pages for these kids, then you might've noticed that two of them reside in a point in the future where the planet is a water filled wasteland with no human life upon it. This is because Her Imperious Condescension (or the Condese for short) took over the planet and accelerated global warming. The Condese made many attempts to make Earth B more like Alternia, breeding lusii (as seen upon the island Jake English lives), covering the planet in water (her blood type of troll is typically aquatic), and installing juggalos as her puppet presidents (the highest ranked land dwelling trolls in the hemospectrum practice an ICP based religion and her second in command is one of these trolls).

The Condese in order to better take over the Earth assumes the identity of Betty Crocker and becomes known as the Batterwitch, using the company to slowly gain power and strengh. The company releases a great deal of tech, most notably the tiara top which is used to mind control Jane Crocker, the sendificator that Dirk Strider uses to behead himself, and the handheld appearifier which crushes Roxy Lalonde's cat with a book.

The Condese's subtle takeover of Earth is not entirely unopposed as Dave Strider, a famous director of the popular Sweet Bro and Hella Jeff movies (and Dirk Strider's bro), and Rose Lalonde, famous author of the Complacency Learned series, ensure earth does not go down without a fight. Dave Strider's movies contined subtle protest against the Condese, trying to alert the people to what was going on. Though it was all for naught Strider and Lalonde go down fighing, killing the Condese's High Subjugalator Guy Fieri and the puppet co-Presidents the Insane Clown Posse, though Strider and Lalonde themselves die in the process. Before going out however, Lalonde with her seer powers forsees the arival of Roxy Lalonde and Dirk Strider many years in the future and the children are adaquately prepared for with each adult preparing for their own child shelter, supplies and anything else they think the children who will have to raise themselves will need.

On the opposite end of this revolutionary spectrum, John Egbert and Jade Harley are adopted by the Condese to be groomed for her own needs. Harley escapes alone and takes on the last name of English in an act of defiance and an attempt to scare off the Condese due to the fact that she knows the Condese is working under someone with the name of English who she does not like, the knewly named Jade English then starts a company rival to the Crocker corperation before getting run out of buisness and exiled with her adoptive grandchild Jake. After not returning home for days, jake finds her corpse with three holes in it and she is cremated and left in a temple. John Egbert on the other hand becomes John Crocker, a comedian and a kind man who partakes in many comedic roles and could be considered legendary.
