What is a scratch?

Within the world of homestuck, a last ditch effort to make things go right when everything has gone to shit is to cause a scratch. A scratch is a complete reset to an indeterminate point in the past and is done as a last resort in a doomed session to attempt to gain more favorable conditions. It also alters the roles of parent and guardian, swapping them in the new universe and erasing all traces of the past session players included unless they find a way to escape the session and travel to he new one. Each time player is given a device to activate a scratch within their planet, the beat mesa in the Land of Heat and Clockwork for example is the device used for the beta kid's scratch. The construct needs to be literally scratched with very sharp needles which are the quills of Echidna.
