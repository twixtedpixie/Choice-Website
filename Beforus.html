Beforus was a very different society to Alternia. Beforus was peaceful with each higher member of the hemospectrum being duty bound to look after those below them. Culling on this planet meant to take an individual under your care to look after and nurture. The Trolls upon this planet who played the game being from a peaceful planet were ill equiped for the game and failed. They then made a deal with an entity who taught them how to perform a scratch and promised that the new players would be born into a world that would fully prepare them to play the game to victory, this world being manipulated by Doc Scratch into being vicious, violent, prejudied, ruthless, and very keen on teaching their young the importance of architectural design. Then as the scratch was happening, in order to escape it Meenah Piexies (alternate Condesce) kills everyone at just the right moment so even their god tier players had no time to revive, thereby saving all of them from being erased from existance.

The roles of the main trolls within this world are not revealed within the main homestuck story and I don't know this information well enough or remember where it is adaquately to be able to disclose it. Look it up yourself if you're interested cause I know it's out there officially.
